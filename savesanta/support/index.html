Save Santa support

Help for grown-ups. Save Santa: Christmas Maze is a Christmas tilt-maze game for families and young children, on iPhone (it also plays on iPad) and Android phones. If you need a hand, you are in the right place.

Contact us

studio@si187apps.com
We read every message and will get back to you.

Top five quick answers

1. How do we restore the Christmas Pass on a new phone?

Use the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase. Open the app, pass the grown-ups check and tap “Restore Purchases”.

2. The Christmas Pass did not unlock after buying.

Close and reopen the app, then use “Restore Purchases” (behind the grown-ups check). If it still does not appear, email us and tell us which phone you use.

3. A purchase is waiting for approval.

With Ask to Buy or family approval, the family organiser must approve it in the App Store or Google Play. After that, reopen the app and use “Restore Purchases” if it has not appeared.

4. Can we share the Christmas Pass with the family?

On iPhone and iPad, yes, through Apple Family Sharing.

5. What do you know about us?

Nothing that identifies you. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy.

More answers are in the full FAQ.

What is Save Santa?

Santa has been chained up in the icy maze. A child tilts the phone to roll Rudolph through the maze to Santa and break the chains, and Rudolph’s glowing nose lights the way. There are four difficulty tiers (Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme), a Quick Game and an optional untimed “No Rush” mode. There are no ads, no tracking and no accounts.

Purchases and restoring them

Save Santa is free to download and Easy is free. A one-time Christmas Pass unlocks Medium, Hard, Extreme and the Christmas finale. Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, so we never see your payment details, and a grown-ups check sits in front of them.

Changed phone or reinstalled the app? Use Restore Purchases. It sits behind the grown-ups check, so a grown-up needs to pass it first. Restore uses the same App Store or Google Play account that made the purchase.

Still stuck?

Email us at studio@si187apps.com and we will get back to you. It helps to tell us which phone you use and what you were doing when it happened.
