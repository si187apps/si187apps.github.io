Save Santa privacy policy

Save Santa: Christmas Maze is a game for children, designed for families. This page explains in plain English what the app does with information, and what it does not do.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

Save Santa is published by si187 Enterprises Ltd, trading as si187 Apps (company number 15126475). Our registered office is 2 Wheeleys Road, Edgbaston, Birmingham B15 2LD. It is available on iPhone (it also plays on iPad) and Android phones. You can contact us at studio@si187apps.com.

The short version

No accounts

There is no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, location or contacts.

No ads

There are no ads in Save Santa, and no ad software in the app.

No analytics, no tracking

There is no analytics and no tracking software in the app.

No personal data collected

We do not collect personal data from you or your child, and there is nothing for us to sell or share.

Payments are Apple’s and Google’s

We never see your card or payment details.

A grown-ups check

A grown-ups check sits in front of purchases and links that leave the game.

What stays on the device

Your game progress, your trophies (“Best Rescues”) and the game settings are stored on your device, inside the app. They are not sent to us. The game uses your phone’s motion sensors only to read how you tilt it, so that Rudolph can roll. That reading is used on the phone as you play and is never stored or sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes the information held on the device.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you or your child. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not send personal data to us or to anyone else. We do not hold a profile of you, so there is nothing for us to look up, sell or share.

Purchases

Save Santa is free to download, and Easy is free. A one-time Christmas Pass, bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play, unlocks Medium, Hard, Extreme and the Christmas finale. Purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your name, card or payment details. On iPhone and iPad the Christmas Pass can be shared through Apple Family Sharing, which Apple manages. The grown-ups check sits in front of purchases so that children do not reach the shop by accident.

Sharing your rescue

The finale (“I Saved Santa!”) lets you share your rescue. Sharing uses your phone’s own share sheet, so you choose where it goes. We do not receive what you share, and we do not know who you share it with.

Links out of the game

Links that take you out of the game, for example to our website, sit behind the grown-ups check. When you open a link you leave the app, and the website or app you reach is covered by its own privacy policy. Our website’s own policy is on the studio privacy page.

Information held by the stores

Apple and Google run the App Store and Google Play, and they handle your account, downloads and purchases under their own privacy policies. They may give us summary reports about sales and downloads in aggregate. Those reports do not identify you or your child.

Children’s privacy

Save Santa is designed for children and for families. We follow the principles of the UK Children’s Code (the Age Appropriate Design Code) and COPPA. Children never need an account, there are no ads, there is no tracking, purchases and links out sit behind a grown-ups check, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

How long we keep information

Because we do not collect personal information through the app, there is nothing for us to keep or delete. If you email us, we keep your message only for as long as needed to help you, and we will delete it on request.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us for access to any information we hold about you, ask us to delete it, and complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. To use any of these rights, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app does with information, we will update this page and the date at the top. This version was last updated on 16 November 2026.
