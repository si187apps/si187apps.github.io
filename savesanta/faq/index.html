Getting started

What is Save Santa: Christmas Maze?

Save Santa is a Christmas tilt-maze game for families and young children, made by si187 Apps. Santa has been chained up in the icy maze. You tilt your phone to roll Rudolph through the maze, reach Santa and break the chains. Rudolph’s glowing nose lights the way.

When can we get it?

Save Santa launches in December 2026. The iPhone version is on the App Store and the Android version is coming to Google Play at launch. For news, see the studio updates.

How do you play?

Tilt your phone to roll Rudolph through the icy maze, find Santa and break his chains. Pick Easy, Medium, Hard or Extreme, or jump into a Quick Game. If you get stuck, Rudolph’s Reindeer Help makes his glow light the way. You can also try a small playable demo right here on the website.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone (it also plays on iPad) and Android phones.

What is the “No Rush” mode?

An optional untimed mode, for children (or grown-ups) who would rather take their time and enjoy the maze without a clock.

What is Quick Game?

A quick way to jump straight into a maze when you only have a minute.

What are the four difficulty tiers?

Easy, Medium, Hard and Extreme. Easy is free to play. A one-time Christmas Pass unlocks Medium, Hard, Extreme and the Christmas finale.

Rescues, trophies and the finale

What happens when you rescue Santa?

Each time you reach Santa and break the chains, that counts as a rescue. Rescue Santa 5 times in a row and you level up.

What are the trophies?

Trophies are kept under “Best Rescues”, so you can see how well you have done on each difficulty.

What is the Christmas finale?

A big celebration screen, “I Saved Santa!”, with a way to share your rescue. The finale is part of the Christmas Pass.

How does sharing work?

The share button uses your phone’s own share sheet, so you choose who to send it to and which app to use. We do not receive anything you share.

Buying and restoring

Is Save Santa free?

Yes, it is free to download, and Easy is free to play. There are no ads.

What does the Christmas Pass unlock?

Medium, Hard and Extreme, plus the Christmas finale. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. The store shows the price in your local currency before you buy.

Can my child buy something by accident?

No. A grown-ups check sits in front of purchases and in front of links that leave the game, and the App Store and Google Play have their own family controls on top.

Does Family Sharing work?

On iPhone and iPad, yes. The Christmas Pass can be shared with your family through Apple Family Sharing.

We got a new phone. How do we get the Christmas Pass back?

Sign in to the same Apple or Google account, open the app, pass the grown-ups check and tap “Restore Purchases”.

How do we get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone and iPad, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use your order history in Google Play. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, but we are happy to help if you are unsure what to do. Email studio@si187apps.com.

Safety and privacy for parents

Are there ads?

No. There are no ads in Save Santa.

Is anything tracked?

No. There is no analytics and no tracking software in the app.

Do we need to make an account?

No. There are no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for a child’s name, email address or location.

What information do you collect?

None that identifies you or your child. See the privacy policy for the full details.

What is the grown-ups check?

A short check in front of purchases and links out of the game, so children cannot wander into the shop or onto a website by accident.

Troubleshooting

The Christmas Pass did not unlock after buying.

Close and reopen the app, then pass the grown-ups check and tap “Restore Purchases”. Use the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase. If it still does not appear, email us.

A purchase is waiting for approval.

With Ask to Buy or family approval, the family organiser must approve it in the App Store or Google Play. After that, reopen the app and use “Restore Purchases” if it has not appeared.

Tilting does not feel right.

Hold the phone comfortably and lean it gently in the direction you want Rudolph to roll. If something still feels wrong, email us with your phone model.

How do we contact you?

Email studio@si187apps.com. We read every message.

Still have a question?

Email us and we will get back to you.
