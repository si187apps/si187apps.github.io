Can you get Rudolph to Santa?

This small maze is just a taste of the game. Rudolph’s glowing nose lights the way, so follow the glow, reach Santa and break his chains.

  • Keyboard: click the maze, then use the arrow keys or W, A, S, D.
  • Touch or mouse: tap “Tap to play”, then drag in the direction you want Rudolph to roll.
  • On a phone: tap “Steer by tilting” to roll Rudolph by leaning your phone, like the real game.
  • Stuck? Switch on Reindeer Help and his glow reaches further.

The full game has four difficulty tiers, a Quick Game, an untimed No Rush mode, trophies and a Christmas finale.

How to play

How it works

Easy for little hands, with a Christmas story to rescue.

  1. Tilt to move

    Lean your phone to roll Rudolph through the icy maze. Little ones get the hang of it in a minute.

  2. Find Santa

    Reach Santa at the end of the maze and break the chains that hold him.

  3. Follow the glow

    Rudolph’s glowing nose lights the way. Stuck? Reindeer Help makes his glow light the way for you.

  4. Rescue 5 in a row

    Save Santa 5 times in a row to level up, and watch your trophies fill up under “Best Rescues”.

Pick your challenge. Choose Easy, Medium, Hard or Extreme, or jump straight into a Quick Game. Prefer no clock? Try the optional untimed “No Rush” mode.

A peek inside

Quick answers

Is Save Santa free?

It is free to download and Easy is free to play. A one-time Christmas Pass unlocks Medium, Hard, Extreme and the Christmas finale. There are no ads.

Can my child buy something by accident?

No. A grown-ups check sits in front of purchases and links out of the game, and the App Store and Google Play have their own family controls on top.

Does it track my child or need an account?

No. There is no analytics, no tracking and no accounts. We do not collect personal data.

Which phones does it run on?

iPhone (it also plays on iPad) and Android phones.
