Cocktail attire for men, explained

Smart evening: a step below black tie.

By si187 Apps · 6 November 2026 · 2 min read

Cocktail attire turns up on invites for evening parties, launches and some weddings. It means dressing up for the evening, with more freedom than black tie.

What to wear, piece by piece

Jacket
A dark suit, or a dark blazer with contrasting trousers. Velvet or textured jackets suit winter parties.
Shirt
A crisp shirt; a tie is optional. A fine roll neck under a suit jacket is a modern alternative.
Trousers
Suit trousers or dark wool trousers.
Shoes
Smart leather shoes: Oxfords, loafers or sleek Chelsea boots, polished.
Extras
A pocket square, a good watch and a smart coat for the journey.

What to avoid

  • Daytime casual pieces such as chinos and polos.
  • Sportswear or trainers.
  • Going full black tie unless you're asked to.

Two ways to wear it

Keep it classic

Charcoal suit, white shirt, dark silk tie and black Oxfords.

Push it a little

Navy velvet blazer, black roll neck, black trousers and black suede loafers.

Quick questions

Tie or no tie?

Either. A tie is more formal; an open collar or roll neck is more relaxed. Take your cue from the venue and the time.

Is a black suit okay?

Yes. A cocktail party is one of the best places to wear one.

What if it says cocktail on a wedding invite?

Start with a dark suit and tie. You can lose the tie once the dancing starts.
