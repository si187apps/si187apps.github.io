Smart casual for men, explained

Relaxed but clearly put together. The most common dress code, and the most confusing.

By si187 Apps · 6 November 2026 · 2 min read

Smart casual turns up on invites for everything from team lunches to fortieth birthdays, and it trips people up because it's so broad. Think of it like this: no suit and tie, but nothing you'd wear to the gym. One smart piece and one relaxed piece usually gets it right.

What to wear, piece by piece

Jacket
A blazer, overshirt or good knit as your top layer. An unstructured blazer in navy, grey or brown is the most useful thing you can own.
Top
A casual shirt, a knitted polo, a fine knit, or a plain T-shirt under a blazer.
Trousers
Chinos, wool trousers or dark jeans.
Shoes
Loafers, Chelsea boots, Derbies or clean minimal leather trainers.
Extras
A belt that matches your shoes. No tie needed.

What to avoid

  • Gym trainers, hoodies and sports tops.
  • A full suit and tie, which is too formal.
  • Shorts, unless it's a summer daytime event and you know the crowd.

Two ways to wear it

Keep it classic

Navy blazer, light blue Oxford shirt, stone chinos and brown suede loafers.

Push it a little

Brown checked overshirt worn open over a cream roll neck, charcoal trousers and white leather trainers.

Quick questions

Are jeans smart casual?

Yes, if they're dark, well-fitting and unfaded. Pair them with a smarter top layer such as a blazer or overshirt.

Can I wear a suit?

A suit with a knit or open shirt can work, but it can look overdressed. A blazer with contrasting trousers sits better.

What's the easiest smart casual formula?

A navy blazer, a plain shirt or knit, dark trousers or jeans, and clean leather shoes. Then change one piece at a time to suit the event.
