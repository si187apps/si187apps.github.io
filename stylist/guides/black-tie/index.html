Black tie for men, explained

Evening formal: the most formal most men ever need.

By si187 Apps · 6 November 2026 · 2 min read

Black tie has clear rules, which is good news: get a few essentials right and you'll look the part. It's for evening events such as galas, formal dinners and some weddings. If the invite says black tie optional, a dark suit and tie is acceptable, but a dinner suit is still the better choice.

What to wear, piece by piece

Jacket
A dinner suit (tuxedo) jacket in black or midnight navy, usually single-breasted with satin or grosgrain lapels. Peak and shawl lapels both work.
Shirt
A white dress shirt with a turn-down collar and a pleated or plain front. Double cuffs with cufflinks are the norm.
Trousers
Matching dinner suit trousers, usually with a braid down the side. No belt: side adjusters or braces instead.
Shoes
Black patent or highly polished black leather Oxfords, with black dress socks.
Extras
A black bow tie you tie yourself (ten minutes of practice; it is what the dress code expects), a white pocket square, and a dark overcoat for the journey. Optionally a cummerbund or a low-cut evening waistcoat, never both.

What to avoid

  • A normal suit and long tie, unless the invite says optional.
  • Brown shoes.
  • A belt with dinner trousers.
  • Novelty bow ties at a formal event.

Two ways to wear it

Keep it classic

Black dinner suit with satin peak lapels, white pleated dress shirt, black silk bow tie, black patent Oxfords and a white pocket square.

Push it a little

Midnight navy dinner suit with shawl lapels, or a velvet dinner jacket in deep green or burgundy with black dinner trousers. Keep the shirt, bow tie and shoes classic.

Quick questions

Should I hire or buy?

If you'll go to more than a couple of black-tie events, buying is usually worth it, and a dinner suit that fits you always looks better than a hired one.

Can I wear a long tie?

Not at true black tie. The bow tie is the point.

What does creative black tie mean?

The rules relax a little: a velvet jacket, a patterned bow tie, or a black roll neck instead of the shirt and bow tie are all fair game.
