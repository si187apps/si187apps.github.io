How it works

Occasion in.
Priced outfit out.

01

Tell us the plan

The occasion, where and when, the look you're after, the shops you like, and your total budget.

02

Three complete outfits

Picked only from retailer stock, dressed for the occasion and the forecast, each totalled against your budget.

03

Make it yours

Swap any piece, or ask for a cheaper version of the whole look. The total updates as you go.

04

Buy direct

Each piece links to the retailer's own product page. You buy from them, at their price.

Inside the app

Every part of the trip.
One list.

Tell Kitted where you're going and it splits the trip into the moments that need different clothes. Pick a look for each, then see every piece once, with the total.

Screens from the beta app using our test catalogue: items, prices and retailers are made up, and the place details are an example. Real retailer stock and shop links arrive as retailers connect.

The fine print, up front

Straight with you.

We earn commission

When you buy through our links, the retailer pays us a commission. Your price doesn't change, and we say so before any shop link.

Your answers, handled with care

No account and no photos. Your answers are sent to Google Gemini, an AI service, to build your outfits; we don't store them. Privacy notice.

Starting small

Menswear, UK only, 18 and over. A closed beta first, so we get it right before we open the doors.
