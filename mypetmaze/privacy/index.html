My Pet Maze privacy policy

My Pet Maze is a game for children, designed for families. This page explains in plain English what the app does with information, what it does not do, and how to ask us to delete anything we hold.

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Who we are

My Pet Maze is published by si187 Enterprises Ltd, trading as si187 Apps (company number 15126475). Our registered office is 2 Wheeleys Road, Edgbaston, Birmingham B15 2LD. It is available on iPhone, iPad and Android. We are the controller of the limited information described below. You can contact us at studio@si187apps.com.

The short version

No accounts

There is no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, location or contacts.

No tracking

There is no analytics, no tracking and no third-party analytics or tracking software in the app.

Photos are not kept

If a grown-up uses the own-pet feature, the photo is used to draw a cartoon and is not stored. We keep only the finished cartoon.

Grown-ups only for the sensitive bits

A grown-ups maths question (a parental gate) protects purchases, restoring purchases, the photo feature and the link to our website.

Payments are Apple's and Google's

We never see your card or payment details.

No selling, no sharing, no marketing

We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for marketing.

What stays on the device

The name your child gives their pet, their progress in the game, their trophies and any cartoon of their pet are stored on your device, inside the app. The pet’s name stays on the device only: it is never sent to us. Deleting the app removes this on-device information.

The optional “own pet” photo feature

This feature is optional and sits behind the grown-ups maths question. It is for grown-ups to use, not children. If you choose to use it, this is what happens:

  • You pick the photo. A grown-up chooses a photo of their pet from the device’s photo library. The app does not use the camera.
  • The photo is sent securely. The photo is resized on the device and sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server, which runs on Google Cloud Run in the London region (europe-west2). It is sent together with the kind of pet you chose (for example “dog”) and the identifier of your store purchase (explained below).
  • An AI draws a cartoon. Our server uses Google’s Gemini AI API to draw a cartoon of the pet. Google acts as our processor (sub-processor) under the Gemini API terms. We use Google’s paid Gemini API service, under which Google does not use your photo to train its AI models.
  • We do not keep the photo. The photo is processed in memory and is not stored by us, and it is never written to our logs. It is not used to identify anyone.
  • You check the result. The family sees the cartoon and chooses whether it looks like their pet. There are a limited number of goes per pet, so we count them.

Tip: please choose a photo that shows just the pet, with no people in it.

What we do keep, and why

We keep a small amount of information so that your pet can come back if you delete and reinstall the app, or use a new phone signed in to the same Apple or Google account. It is tied to the store purchase, not to a person.

Purchases and the purchase identifier

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details, and prices only appear after the grown-ups maths question. To tie a cartoon to a purchase, the app uses an identifier the store gives for that purchase (Apple’s original transaction ID on iPhone and iPad, or the Google Play purchase token on Android). It identifies the purchase, not you: we do not receive your name or account details with it.

How we use information

Only to draw and restore your pet’s cartoon, to limit the number of goes, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell or share your data, we do not use it for marketing, and we do not use it to train AI models.

Who else handles it

  • Google Cloud hosts our server and stores the cartoon and usage record.
  • Google’s Gemini API draws the cartoon from the photo.
  • Apple and Google process payments and purchase restores.

We do not share information with anyone else.

Children's privacy

My Pet Maze is designed for children and for families. We follow the principles of the UK Children’s Code (the Age Appropriate Design Code) and COPPA. Children never see prices, the shop and the photo feature sit behind a grown-ups maths question, there are no accounts, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The photo feature is for grown-ups. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep the stored cartoon and its usage record until you ask us to delete them, so your pet can always come back. Deleting the app removes the information held on the device itself, but not the copy we keep for restoring.

To have it deleted: email studio@si187apps.com, attach a screenshot of your pet’s cartoon (from the Your pets screen) and tell us roughly when you made it. Because we never hold names or emails, the cartoon itself is how we find your record. We match the cartoon, delete it together with its usage record, and confirm by email within 30 days. After that the pet can no longer be restored on a new phone, but your purchases still restore as normal through Apple or Google.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us for access to the information we hold, ask us to delete it, and complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. To use any of these rights, email us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we do with information, we will update this page and the date at the top. This version was last updated on 15 November 2026.
