SI187 APPS · MY PET MAZE

PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date · 11 November 2026

My Pet Maze is a game for children, designed for families. This page explains in plain English what the app does with information, what it does not do, and how to ask us to delete anything we hold.

My Pet Maze is published by si187 Enterprises Ltd, trading as si187apps (2 Wheeleys Road, Edgbaston, Birmingham, B15 2LD). It is available on iOS and Android. We are the controller of the limited information described below.

The short version

  • No ads, no tracking There are no adverts, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising ID and no third-party ad or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • No accounts There is no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, location or contacts.
  • Grown-ups only for the sensitive bits A grown-ups maths question (a parental gate) protects purchases, restoring purchases, the photo feature and the link to our website.
  • No selling, no sharing, no marketing We do not sell or share your data, and we do not use it for marketing.

What stays on the device

The name your child gives their pet, their progress in the game and any cartoon of their pet are stored on your device, inside the app. The pet's name stays on the device only: it is never sent to us. Deleting the app removes this on-device information.

The optional “Meet your pet” photo feature

This feature is optional and sits behind the grown-ups maths question. It is for grown-ups to use, not children. If you choose to use it:

  • You pick the photo A grown-up chooses a photo of their pet from the device's photo library. The app does not use the camera.
  • The photo is sent securely The photo is resized and sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server, which runs on Google Cloud Run in the London region (europe-west2).
  • An AI draws a cartoon Our server uses Google's Gemini AI API to draw a cartoon of the pet. Google acts as our processor (sub-processor) under the Gemini API terms.
  • We do not keep the photo The photo is processed in memory and is not stored by us. It is not used to identify anyone.

Tip: please choose a photo that shows just the pet, with no people in it.

What we do keep

  • The finished cartoon Only the finished cartoon of the pet (an image) is stored, in Google Cloud Storage, so that it can be restored on a new device. It is keyed by a one-way hash of the app-store purchase identifier, so we cannot read your purchase details from it.
  • A small usage record The number of cartoon attempts and the date of any restore, keyed by the store purchase identifier. We use this only to limit the number of attempts and to make restore work.
  • Server logs Our server provider's standard logs may record IP addresses for security and abuse prevention. We do not use them for anything else.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. Prices only appear after the grown-ups maths question.

How we use information

Only to draw and restore your pet's cartoon, to limit the number of attempts, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell or share your data, we do not use it for marketing, and we do not use it to train AI models.

How long we keep it

We keep the stored cartoon and usage record until you ask us to delete them. To ask, email studio@si187apps.com and tell us roughly when you bought the app on that device, so we can find the right record. We will delete the stored cartoon and record within 30 days.

Children's privacy

My Pet Maze is designed for children and for families. We follow the principles of COPPA and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code. The photo feature is for grown-ups, behind the parental gate, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us for access to the information we hold, ask us to delete it, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk. To use any of these rights, email us using the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change what we do with information, we will update this page and the effective date above. Effective date: 11 November 2026.
