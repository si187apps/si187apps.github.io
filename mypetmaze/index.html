My Pet Maze

Tilt the phone. Guide your pet. Read their very own storybook.

A gentle maze and storybook game for children, for iPhone, iPad and Android. Tilt your phone to lead a pet through the hedge maze, gather the treats and find the magic book. Every win turns the page on the pet's own story. And a grown-up can turn a photo of your real pet into the hero.

Your pet, the hero

Turn your pet into the hero

A grown-up picks a photo of the family pet, and the game draws them as a storybook cartoon. Then your very own pet steps into the maze and the story.

  • A grown-up chooses a photo of the pet: just the pet, in bright light, with the face showing.
  • The photo is sent securely to our server, which draws the cartoon.
  • You see the reveal and answer “Does this look like your pet?” Not quite right? Try another photo (each pet has a limited number of goes).
  • Give your pet a name, and their story begins.

We keep only the finished cartoon, never the photo. That way your pet can come back if you get a new phone. Read how it works in our privacy policy.

How to play

How it works

Easy for little hands, with a story that grows as they play.

  1. Tilt to steer

    Tilt the phone and your pet walks through the hedge maze. Little ones get the hang of it in a minute.

  2. Collect the treats

    Gather every treat along the way. Stuck? Tap “Show Me!” for a hint.

  3. Find the magic book

    Reach the magic book at the end of the maze to win, and earn your stars.

  4. Turn the page

    Each win moves your pet's storybook on. Book 1 has six illustrated chapters with story pages to read together, and there are trophies to collect.

Pick your challenge. Choose Easy, Just Right, Tricky or Super, or jump straight into a Quick Game.

Quick answers

Is My Pet Maze free?

It is free to download. Red the dog and his whole Book 1 story are free to play through once. After that, a grown-up can unlock more play with a one-off purchase. There are no subscriptions.

What happens to my pet’s photo?

A grown-up chooses it, it is sent securely to our server to draw the cartoon, and we do not keep it. We keep only the finished cartoon, so your pet can come back on a new phone.

Can my child buy something by accident?

No. Every shop screen sits behind a “Grown-ups only!” maths question, children’s screens never show prices, and the App Store and Google Play have their own family controls on top.

We got a new phone. Where is our pet?

Open the app, tap “Restore purchases” (behind the grown-ups question), then tap “+ Add a pet” and wait for “Looking for your pet…” Your pet comes back with a “Welcome back!”
