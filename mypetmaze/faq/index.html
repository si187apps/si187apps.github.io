Getting started

What is My Pet Maze?

My Pet Maze is a maze and storybook game for children, made by si187 Apps for iPhone, iPad and Android. You tilt the phone to guide a pet through a hedge maze, collect treats and reach the magic book. Each win moves the pet’s storybook on.

When can we get it?

It is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. We are preparing it for both stores now. There is no date yet, but the studio updates are the place to hear first.

How do you play?

Tilt the phone to steer your pet through the maze, collect the treats and reach the magic book. If you get stuck, tap “Show Me!” for a hint. You can pick Easy, Just Right, Tricky or Super, or play a Quick Game. Wins unlock story chapters, and there are trophies to collect.

What is in the free game?

Red the dog and his whole Book 1 story, played through once: all six illustrated chapters, right to “The End”. After that, a grown-up can unlock more play.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and Android.

Do we need an internet connection?

You need one for anything that involves the store or our server: buying, restoring purchases, drawing your own pet’s cartoon, and bringing a pet back after a reinstall or on a new phone.

Your own pet and photos

How does the “own pet” feature work?

A grown-up chooses a photo of the pet. The photo is sent securely to our server, which draws a storybook cartoon of the pet. The family then checks the reveal (“Does this look like your pet?”), can try another photo if it is not quite right, and finally gives the pet a name. Then the pet’s story begins.

What makes a good photo?

A photo of just the pet, in bright light, with the face showing. Please choose a photo with no people in it. The photo is picked from the device’s photo library.

The cartoon does not look like our pet. What can we do?

Choose “Try another photo”. A clearer picture in bright light, taken at the pet’s level and showing the face, usually helps. Each pet has a limited number of goes, and the app tells you how many are left.

What if the photo is not of the kind of pet we chose?

If the picture does not show a pet of that kind, we cannot draw it and the app asks for a different photo. That try does not use up one of your goes.

Which pets can be drawn?

The eight kinds of storybook pet: dog, cat, rabbit, bird, rodents, horse, tortoises and fish.

How many of our own pets can we have?

Up to five. Your first “Add my pet” purchase gives you one place for your own pet, and each further purchase adds another, up to five.

Do you keep our photo?

No. The photo is used to draw the cartoon and is not stored by us. We keep only the finished cartoon. See the privacy policy for the full details.

Can children name their pets?

Yes. Pets are named after the reveal. The name is kept on your device only and is never sent to us. The free storybook dog starts out called Red.

Buying and restoring

How much does it cost?

The game is free to download. “Add my pet” is a one-off purchase of £1.49 (the store shows the price in your local currency, and it varies by country). Your first purchase unlocks all seven other storybook animals for good and gives you one place for your own pet. Each further £1.49 adds another own-pet place, up to five.

Is there a subscription?

No. There are no subscriptions. Every purchase is a one-off that you keep, and you can restore it with “Restore purchases”.

Does the first purchase unlock everything?

It unlocks the seven other storybook animals (cat, rabbit, bird, rodents, horse, tortoises and fish) for good, and one place for your own pet. More own-pet places are separate £1.49 purchases.

How do we restore a purchase?

Open the app, go to the shop through the grown-ups maths question, and tap “Restore purchases”. Use the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase.

We got a new phone, or reinstalled the app. Will our pet come back?

Yes. Sign in to the same Apple or Google account, use “Restore purchases”, then tap “+ Add a pet” and wait for “Looking for your pet…” Your pet’s cartoon is stored against the purchase, so it comes back with a “Welcome back!”

We use Ask to Buy or family approval. Does that work?

Yes. Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play, so their family controls apply as normal. The request goes to the family organiser, and once it is approved the purchase unlocks. If it does not show up, open the app and use “Restore purchases”.

How do we get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone and iPad, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use your order history in Google Play. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, but we are happy to help if you are unsure what to do.

Safety and privacy for parents

Can my child buy something by accident?

No. Every shop screen sits behind a “Grown-ups only!” maths question, and children’s screens never show prices. Purchases then go through the App Store or Google Play, which add their own family controls such as Ask to Buy.

Do you collect my child’s name or email?

No. There are no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for names, email addresses, location or contacts, and a pet’s name stays on your device.

What do you keep, and why?

Only if you use the own-pet feature: the finished cartoon (never the photo), the kind of pet, and a small record of how many goes have been used. They are tied to the purchase, not to a person, so your pet can come back after a reinstall or on a new phone. Everything is explained in the privacy policy.

Is there tracking?

No. The app has no analytics and no tracking, and we do not sell or share your data or use it for marketing.

How do we ask for our data to be deleted?

Email studio@si187apps.com and attach a screenshot of your pet’s cartoon (from the Your pets screen) and tell us roughly when you made it. Because we never hold names or emails, the cartoon itself is how we find your record. We delete the stored cartoon and its record and confirm by email within 30 days. Deleting the app removes everything on the device itself.

Troubleshooting

The shop will not load.

Check that the device is online and that you are signed in to the App Store (iPhone and iPad) or Google Play (Android). Then close the app, open it again and retry. If it still will not load, wait a few minutes and try again, as the store may be busy. Still stuck? Email studio@si187apps.com.

Our pet did not come back after a reinstall or new phone.

Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase and use “Restore purchases”. Then tap “+ Add a pet” and give the app a moment: wait for “Looking for your pet…” to finish. If the app says it cannot find your pet’s drawings, follow the steps on screen. If you are still stuck, email us and tell us roughly when you bought the app.

We bought something and it has not unlocked.

Open the app, go to the shop through the grown-ups question and use “Restore purchases”. Make sure you are on the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase.

A purchase is waiting for approval.

If your family uses Ask to Buy or family approval, the family organiser needs to approve the request in the App Store or Google Play first. Once they have, reopen the app and use “Restore purchases” if it has not appeared.

Something else is wrong.

Email studio@si187apps.com and tell us what happened, which device you use, and roughly when you bought the app if it is about a purchase. We read every message.

Still stuck?

Email studio@si187apps.com and we will get back to you. See also Support and the privacy policy.
