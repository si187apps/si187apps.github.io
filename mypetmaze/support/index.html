My Pet Maze support

Help for grown-ups. My Pet Maze is a maze and storybook game for children, designed for families, on iPhone, iPad and Android. If you need a hand, you are in the right place.

Contact us

studio@si187apps.com
We read every message and will get back to you.

Top five quick answers

1. How do we restore a purchase or get our pet back on a new phone?

Use the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase. Open the app, answer the grown-ups maths question, and tap “Restore purchases”. Then tap “+ Add a pet” and wait for “Looking for your pet…” Your pet comes back with a “Welcome back!”

2. The shop will not load.

Check the device is online and that you are signed in to the App Store or Google Play, then close and reopen the app. If it still will not load, try again in a few minutes.

3. A purchase is waiting for approval.

With Ask to Buy or family approval, the family organiser must approve it in the App Store or Google Play. After that, reopen the app and use “Restore purchases” if it has not appeared.

4. The cartoon does not look like our pet.

Choose “Try another photo”. Pick a clear photo of just the pet, in bright light, with the face showing and no people in it. Each pet has a limited number of goes.

5. How do we ask for our data to be deleted?

Email studio@si187apps.com and attach a screenshot of your pet’s cartoon (from the Your pets screen) and tell us roughly when you made it. Because we never hold names or emails, the cartoon itself is how we find your record. See the privacy policy.

More answers are in the full FAQ.

What is My Pet Maze?

A child guides a pet through hedge mazes by tilting the phone, collects treats and reaches the magic book, and each win moves the pet’s storybook on. There are no accounts. A grown-ups maths question keeps purchases and other grown-up features out of little fingers’ reach.

Purchases and restoring them

Purchases are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play, so we never see your payment details. Prices only appear after the grown-ups maths question.

Bought something before and changed phone or reinstalled the app? Use Restore purchases. It sits behind the grown-ups maths question, so a grown-up needs to answer it first. Restore uses the same App Store or Google Play account that made the purchase. If you have used the own-pet photo feature, your pet’s cartoon comes back too.

Still stuck?

Email us at studio@si187apps.com and we will get back to you. For a data deletion request, tell us roughly when you bought the app.
