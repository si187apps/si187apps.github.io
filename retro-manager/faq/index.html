Frequently asked questions

Everything about Retro Manager: pricing, devices, saves, importing and refunds.

What is Retro Manager?

Retro Manager is a football management game in the style of 2001. You pick a club, pick your team and tactics, make signings and play out seasons. It is made by si187 Apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

When does it launch?

Friday 12 December 2026 on the App Store and Google Play. Leave your email and we will tell you the moment it is live.

How much does it cost?

Your first season is free. After that, the Full Game is a single payment of £4.99 and lets you play on forever. The best value bundle is £6.99 and gives you the Full Game plus 1 import of your own database file. Extra imports are £2.99 each, for example a new season's data, and need the Full Game. Launch week offer: the bundle is £4.99 from Friday 12 to Friday 19 December 2026. Prices are shown in your local currency by the store.

What does the first season include?

The complete game for your first season in the built-in Classic World: squad, tactics, training, transfers, finances, scouting, cups, news and live match days. There are no ads in the way.

When the season ends you can buy the Full Game to keep going. Your save stays on your device either way.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad, and Android phones and tablets. The game is designed to be played with the device on its side (landscape).

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Retro Manager plays offline. You only need a connection to buy the Full Game or an import through the App Store or Google Play.

Where are my saves kept?

On your device, and nowhere else. There is no account and nothing is uploaded. You can keep several saves. If you delete the app, its saves go with it, and moving to a new device means starting again there, so take your usual device backup before you change phones.

What is the Classic World?

A complete, original football world with 4 divisions of invented clubs and thousands of invented players. It is built into the app, so there is nothing to download or set up. Every name in it is fictional.

Can I import my own database file?

Yes. The app can convert a database file you already have, so you can play it in Retro Manager. Importing needs the Full Game. The £6.99 bundle includes 1 import, and each extra import is £2.99. You supply your own file: we do not provide, host or link to any data files.

The app asks for a zip of your database folder (the folder called Data, which has a file called index.dat inside). Our step by step guide shows exactly how to send it to your phone or tablet and import it.

My import did not work. What now?

Check the Something went wrong? section of the import guide. It lists each message the app can show, with the fix. If you are still stuck, email studio@si187apps.com.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are too. On iPhone and iPad, use reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use your order history in Google Play. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, but we are happy to help if you are unsure what to do.

I bought the Full Game and it has not unlocked.

Open the app and use Restore Purchases on the unlock screen. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account that made the purchase. If it still does not appear, email us.

Does the app collect my data?

No. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics. Read the privacy policy for the details.

How do I contact you?

Email studio@si187apps.com. We read every message.
