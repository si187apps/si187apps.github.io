Everything a classic manager does

Run the club

Squad, tactics, training, finances and the board. The whole manager's chair, built to fit a phone or a tablet.

Play it your pace

Pick up for 5 minutes between other things, or settle in for a long evening. Your season is always saved on your device.

A world of its own

The built-in Classic World is entirely fictional, with invented clubs and players, ready to play from the first tap.

  • 4 divisions of clubs, plus cup competitions and European football
  • Player profiles with full attributes, form, contracts and history
  • Transfers, bids, contract offers and a free agent market
  • Tactics with formations, with the ball and without the ball plans
  • Live match days with commentary, stats, ratings and substitutions
  • Scouting, shortlists, player and staff search with filters
  • News, fixtures, results, league tables, records and awards
  • Several saves, kept privately on your own device

  • Android phones and tablets
