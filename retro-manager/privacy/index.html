Retro Manager privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Retro Manager collects no personal data. This page explains what the app does, what it does not do, and how the website treats visitors.

In short

  • No account, no sign in, no email address needed to play.
  • Your saves and databases stay on your device.
  • No ads, no analytics and no crash reporting inside the app.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple and Google.

What the app stores

DataWhere it lives
Your saved careers and settingsOn your device only. We never receive them.
Databases you importOn your device only. Files you choose are read on the device and are not uploaded.
Whether you have bought the Full GameA purchase flag stored on your device, checked against the App Store or Google Play.

What the app does not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or address.
  • No location.
  • No contacts, photos, camera or microphone access.
  • No advertising identifier and no tracking of any kind.
  • No analytics or crash reporting software. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Purchases

Buying the Full Game or an import is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not see or store your payment details. We are told only whether a purchase succeeded, through the store's own system. Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Files you import

When you import a database file, the app opens the file you pick, converts it on your device and keeps the result on your device. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else. You are responsible for having the right to use any file you choose to import.

The help link

The app has a Help importing data button that opens this website in your device's browser. At that point the website's own rules apply, as described below.

This website

The website si187apps.com uses Google Analytics to count visits, but only if you accept analytics in the cookie banner. If you decline, no analytics cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie preferences in the footer. The launch day sign up form sends your email address to our form provider, Formspree, and we use it only to tell you when Retro Manager is available. The site-wide si187 Apps privacy policy covers the website in full.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

All app data is on your device, so deleting the app deletes it. If you signed up for the launch day email and want your address removed, email studio@si187apps.com and we will remove it within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting anything, this page and the App Store and Google Play privacy declarations will change first.
